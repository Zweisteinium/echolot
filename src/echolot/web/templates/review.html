The three answers

Perfect match
It is the song. The file is named and tagged as the song in your list.
Close match
Another version you take for the song, such as an extended mix or a live take. The file keeps the name it really has (you can edit it), so what you play and share is labelled right. Your song counts as covered and is no longer searched; Missing lists these and can search for the perfect match again.
No match
Not the song. The file goes, this download is never taken for the song again, and the song is searched again.

All downloads of a song are on one card, the likeliest first (the audio check, the artist, the length). Taking one as a Perfect or Close match discards the others. A decision is applied by the Library job within a few minutes; until then Revert takes it back.
